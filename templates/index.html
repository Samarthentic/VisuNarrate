        Evaluation Hierarchy & Telemetry Distinction

        1. Evaluation Quality

        Quantitative mathematical performance measured strictly against independent human ground-truth annotations.

        • Detection: IoU >= 0.5, Precision, Recall, mAP@0.5
        • Captions: BLEU-1, BLEU-2, BLEU-4, ROUGE-L F1
        • VQA: Top-1 Exact Match & Soft Semantic Match
        • Temporal: Transition F1 (tolerance ±1.5s)
        2. Model Confidence

        Internal probabilities reported by neural models during individual inference passes.

        • Detector Confidence: [0.25 - 1.0] per bbox
        • Presence Certainty: Confirmed vs. Uncertain
        • Prediction State: Supported vs. Tentative
        • Conflict Count: Contradiction gating
        3. Operational Telemetry

        Empirical runtime hardware and pipeline efficiency measured on Apple Silicon Metal (MPS).

        • Cold Init: Persistent disk to RAM/VRAM
        • Shader JIT Trace: Metal graph compilation
        • Warm Latency: Median & P95 ms
        • Redundancy Filtering: Frame skip ratio (%)
